## 🔍 Research Interests & Focus
My research aims to contribute to a deeper understanding of financial markets, volatility, and the microeconomic factors shaping corporate performance. 

- **Domains:** Financial markets & portfolio management | ESG & firm performance | Volatility spillovers & portfolio hedging
- **Methodologies:** Dynamic connectedness using QVAR, TVP-VAR, GARCH-family, Wavelet, and Transfer Entropy methods.

## 📝 Publications & Academic Work
My research is published in international journals indexed in **ABDC, Web of Science, and Scopus**. I actively participate in global conferences, workshops, and scholarly discussions to bridge the gap between financial theory and data-driven econometric insights.
